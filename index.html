01 / Background

About Me

I hold a PhD in Health Psychology and a Master's degree in Clinical Psychology. My professional background includes clinical work in psychiatric and private-practice settings, alongside teaching, translation, and continued study of psychotherapy and psychoanalysis.

My interests center on the ways personal histories, unconscious processes, and recurring patterns influence relationships and emotional life. My work draws on clinical experience and ongoing engagement with psychoanalytic thought.
