About

I am Soudabeh Gharibi, a Registered Psychotherapist (Qualifying) and Canadian Certified Counsellor (CCC), with a PhD in Health Psychology and an MA in Clinical Psychology. My work brings together clinical practice, psychoanalytic thought, and curiosity about human experience.

Clinical Practice

I offer individual psychotherapy and couples therapy, with space to explore anxiety, relationships, trauma, and the questions that bring us to therapy. Sessions are available in English and Farsi.
