01 / Background

About Me

I hold a PhD in Health Psychology and a Master's degree in Clinical Psychology. My clinical work began in 2013 and has included psychiatric hospital and private-practice settings, alongside teaching, translation, and ongoing study. My professional training includes cognitive behavioural therapy (CBT), schema therapy, contemporary approaches in psychology, modern psychoanalysis, and classical psychoanalysis.

My interests center on the ways personal histories, unconscious processes, and recurring patterns influence relationships and emotional life. My work is informed by experience across different psychotherapeutic approaches and sustained engagement with psychoanalytic thought, particularly Freud and Lacan.

Professional credentials: Registered Psychotherapist (Qualifying), College of Registered Psychotherapists of Ontario (CRPO); Canadian Certified Counsellor (CCC), Canadian Counselling and Psychotherapy Association (CCPA).

02 / Clinical Work

Clinical Practice

My clinical interests include anxiety, trauma, relationship difficulties, and recurring emotional or interpersonal patterns. I approach psychotherapy with attention to each person's particular history, circumstances, and goals.

Relationships

Individual Experience

03 / Ideas & Inquiry

Psychoanalysis

Psychoanalysis offers a way of listening to what may not be immediately evident in our words, choices, and relationships. I am especially interested in repetition, desire, unconscious processes, and the relationship between subjective experience and language.

My ongoing reading and study engage with Freudian and Lacanian psychoanalysis and broader philosophical questions about human life.
