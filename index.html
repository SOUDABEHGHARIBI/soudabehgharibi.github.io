— ABOUT ME —

Psychotherapy, Psychoanalysis, and a Commitment to Deep Listening.

I am Soudabeh Gharibi, currently practicing in Toronto. I hold a PhD in Health Psychology and an MA in Clinical Psychology. I am a Registered Psychotherapist (Qualifying) with the College of Registered Psychotherapists of Ontario (CRPO) and a Canadian Certified Counsellor (CCC).

My clinical training and experience include psychotherapy with adults and adolescents, parenting support, and work with individuals and couples experiencing anxiety, depression, trauma, obsessive-compulsive disorder (OCD), eating disorders, obesity-related psychological concerns, chronic pain, and relationship challenges.

My clinical approach is grounded in psychoanalytic theory and practice. I have received training in modern psychoanalysis, Lacanian psychoanalysis, and psychodynamic approaches, while also drawing on cognitive behavioural therapy (CBT) and schema therapy. I have also been engaged in my own personal psychoanalysis for many years, an experience that continues to inform and deepen my understanding of the therapeutic process.

Alongside my private practice, I have worked in psychiatric hospitals, as well as general hospital settings where my clinical work focused on eating disorders, obesity, and the psychological challenges associated with cancer. I have also served as a psychologist in emergency medical services and provided crisis intervention, psychological support, and group therapy across various clinical and community settings.

My professional activities extend to teaching and translating in the field of mental health, including courses on crisis and disaster psychology and workshops on stress management, anger management, time management, and communication skills.

I offer psychotherapy in both Farsi and English, and I am committed to creating a space where you can speak freely, feel heard, and explore what matters to you.
